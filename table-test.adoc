
= Table Examples

== 1. Simple Table

|===
| Name | Age | City

| Alice
| 30
| New York

| Bob
| 25
| London

| Carol
| 35
| Tokyo
|===

== 2. Header Row (with options)

[options="header"]
|===
| Name | Age | City

| Alice | 30 | New York
| Bob   | 25 | London
| Carol | 35 | Tokyo
|===

== 3. Column Alignment

[cols="<,^,>", options="header"]
|===
| Left | Center | Right

| Alice | 30 | New York
| Bob   | 25 | London
| Carol | 35 | Tokyo
|===

== 4. Cell Formatting

[options="header"]
|===
| Element | Example

| Bold | *bold text*
| Italic | _italic text_
| Monospace | `code`
| Link | https://example.com[Example]
|===

== 5. Colspan and Rowspan

[cols="1,1,1", options="header"]
|===
| Column 1 | Column 2 | Column 3

2+| Spans two columns | Cell
| Cell 1 | Cell 2 | Cell 3

.2+| Spans two rows
| Cell A | Cell B
| Cell C | Cell D
|===

== 6. Nested Formatting

[cols="1,2", options="header"]
|===
| Feature | Status

| Bold | *Works*
| Italic | _Works_
| Code | `works`
| Mixed | *Bold _and italic_ together*
|===
